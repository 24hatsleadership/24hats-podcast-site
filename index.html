The show

Leadership that keeps people at the center.

Useful ideas for the everyday work of leading well. Each episode gives you something to try in an ordinary working relationship, not a theory to admire.

Season 1 ("Leading Is Serving") holds dozens of conversations with business owners, nonprofit leaders, and public servants from Indianapolis and the Southside. Some archive episodes are co-hosted with Chris.
